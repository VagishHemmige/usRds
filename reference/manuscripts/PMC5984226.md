### PMC5984226

[High rates of cancer screening among dialysis patients seen in primary care a cohort study.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5984226/) · 2018 · doi:10.1016/j.pmedr.2018.03.006

Reading: **MT**, 2026-11-15. Main-text reading; figures as images and supplements not inspected; tables not comprehensively checked unless stated.

- **Role / population / unit:** regional incident HD cohort linked to USRDS; individual first cancer-test event and primary-care exposure. [reported Methods]
- **Sources:** network records, claims, CMS-2728, electronic comorbidity extraction and transplant database. Historical monthly dialysis-payment threshold proxies primary payer. [reported Methods]
- **Data needs:** distinguish clinical tests from screening indications; analyze death/transplant as competing events. Claims plus local records build Charlson scores in the first 90 days. [reported Methods]
- **Pitfalls:** excluding short follow-up while starting at dialysis creates a selected survivor cohort. Primary-care classification over follow-up can introduce temporal bias unless exposure timing is resolved. The $675 threshold is historical, not a current default. Five-year secondary follow-up in Methods differs from four-year Results reporting. Prior cancer and transplant candidacy are incompletely observed. [reported Methods; Results; Discussion; inference]
