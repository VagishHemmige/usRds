### PMC8360057

[Erythropoietin treatment and the risk of hip fractures in hemodialysis patients.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8360057/) · 2021 · doi:10.1002/jbmr.4297

Reading: **MT**, 2026-11-15. Main-text reading; figures as images and supplements not inspected; tables not comprehensively checked unless stated.

- **Role / population / unit:** prevalent in-center-HD patients in annual cohorts, 1997–2013; person-month exposure and first fracture within year. [reported Methods]
- **Sources:** core, treatment/payer history, monthly EPO claims; 2013 adds CROWNWeb and Part D. [reported Methods]
- **Data needs:** January entry, prior October–December exposure baseline and six-month comorbidity lookback. Prior-three-month EPO moving average is weight-normalized units/kg/week and time-varying. Main pooled model uses only the first eligible year per person; yearly analyses differ. [reported Methods]
- **Pitfalls:** prescription units, weeks and body weight require explicit conversion. Primary-position fracture claims differ from any-position sensitivity. Missing early BMI excludes years from adjusted analyses; extensive CROWNWeb completeness requirements select a subset. The paper's PTH-availability statement needs release-specific verification. [reported Methods; Results; inference]
- **Locators:** full main text; tables/supplements unread. Annual counts of persons are not automatically unique across years, and an 11-condition summed score is not interchangeable with weighted Charlson scoring.
