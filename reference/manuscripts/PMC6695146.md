### PMC6695146

[Emergency department visits and hospitalizations among hemodialysis patients by day of the week and dialysis schedule in the United States.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6695146/) · 2019 · doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0220966

Reading: **MT**, 2026-11-15. Main-text reading; figures as images and supplements not inspected; tables not comprehensively checked unless stated.

- **Role / population / unit:** Medicare-primary thrice-weekly HD patients in 2013; ED encounter, admission and person-time by weekday and inferred dialysis schedule. [reported Methods]
- **Sources:** inpatient/outpatient claims and CROWNWeb treatment/monthly clinical records. Schedule inferred from first recorded session per treatment period; monthly Kt/V measurement weekday supports a stable-schedule sensitivity analysis. [reported Methods]
- **Data needs:** separate ED alone, same-day ED/admission and admission without same-day ED. Robust Poisson models include weekday-by-schedule indicators; clinical covariates are annual CROWNWeb means. [reported Methods]
- **Pitfalls:** same-day linkage does not establish sequence within a day; exact encounter deduplication and time-at-risk construction need specification. Annual means can include measurements after outcomes. Fixed schedule misclassifies changes; excluding changes or missing Kt/V selects a different subgroup. [reported Methods; Limitations; inference]
- **Locators:** complete main text; diagnostic supplements/tables/images unread. Without session timestamps, missed treatments or clinical records, the weekday associations cannot identify intradialytic causation.
