### PMC10568645

[Plasma Biomarkers and Incident CKD Among Individuals Without Diabetes.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10568645/) · 2023 · doi:10.1016/j.xkme.2023.100719

Reading: **MT**, 2026-11-15. Main-text reading; figures as images and supplements not inspected; tables not comprehensively checked unless stated.

- **Role / population / unit:** ARIC biomarker study using USRDS linkage for dialysis/transplant component of incident CKD; person and sampled case/control biospecimen. [reported Methods]
- **Sources:** visits 4 (1996–1998) and 5 (2011–2013), external plasma biomarkers, creatinine/cystatin and USRDS kidney-failure linkage. [reported Methods]
- **Data needs:** baseline eGFR ≥60 without diabetes; outcome ≥40% decline to <60 or replacement therapy. All eligible cases and a random control sample receive assays; logistic models and risk estimates require recognition of outcome-dependent sampling. [reported Methods]
- **Pitfalls:** requirement for follow-up visit selects survivors and misses progression timing between visits. Biomarkers are external laboratory measurements, not USRDS variables. Absolute-risk calibration uses cohort incidence rather than sampled case fraction; avoid reporting the enriched sample as population risk. [reported Methods; Limitations; inference]
- **Locators:** full main text; tables/supplements/images unread. Registry linkage is a substantive outcome source, but the study does not validate a claims-based CKD phenotype.
