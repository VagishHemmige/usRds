### PMC7056859

[The Marginal Cost of Frailty Among Medicare Patients on Hemodialysis.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7056859/) · 2020 · doi:10.1016/j.ekir.2019.11.020

Reading: **MT**, 2026-11-15. Main-text reading; figures as images and supplements not inspected; tables not comprehensively checked unless stated.

- **Role / population / unit:** ACTIVE/ADIPOSE USRDS special-study linkage; prevalent HD recruited 2009–2011 at 14 centers; person/frailty-assessment interval. [reported Study Design]
- **Files and fields:** A/B/D claim payment amounts, CMS-2728, chart review and prospectively measured Fried frailty. Exact payment columns unreported. [reported Costs; Covariates]
- **Data needs:** retain Medicare-primary periods through twelve months after last frailty assessment; censor at death, transplant or loss. Allocate claims by service-begin date; annualize costs by observed Medicare-primary days. Apply 3% annual adjustment to 2017 dollars; gamma/log GEE with repeated frailty. [reported Methods]
- **Pitfalls:** annualized partial-period spending is not observed twelve-month spending. Start-date allocation differs from prorating claims across boundaries. The fixed 3% adjustment is not CPI-Medical. Main cohort does not require all three Medicare parts; an all-parts-claims sensitivity analysis is separate. Claims presence does not itself prove continuous coverage. [reported Methods; Limitations; inference]
- **Locators:** complete main text; tables/supplements/images unread. Frailty phenotype requires external study measurements, not claims alone.
