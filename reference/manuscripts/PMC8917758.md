### PMC8917758

[Effects of short-term ambient PM<sub>2.5</sub> exposure on cardiovascular disease incidence and mortality among U.S. hemodialysis patients: a retrospective cohort study.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8917758/) · 2022 · doi:10.1186/s12940-022-00836-0

Reading: **MT**, 2026-11-15. Main-text reading; figures as images and supplements not inspected; tables not comprehensively checked unless stated.

- **Role / population / unit:** Medicare-primary adults initiating in-center HD in 2011–2016, followed from month four; person-day linked to environmental exposure. [reported Methods]
- **Sources:** USRDS claims, CMS-2728/2746, clinic ZIP histories, modeled daily PM2.5, NOAA weather and population-weighted geographic aggregation. [reported Methods]
- **Data needs:** carry last clinic ZIP between visits; distinguish same-day exposure, simultaneous distributed lags and moving averages. Complementary-log-log discrete-time hazards use dialysis time, weather and calendar covariates. [reported Methods]
- **Pitfalls:** clinic ZIP proxies residence and personal exposure; “incident CVD” means first follow-up event even with baseline CVD. Mortality and first-CVD analyses have different person-time; do not apply first-CVD censoring to mortality automatically. Cause-of-death and encounter codes use different systems. Equations are incompletely rendered in retrieved text. [reported Methods; Discussion; inference]
- **Locators:** full prose main text; equations, code supplements, tables and image figures unverified. Geographic linkage and lag construction require external data, not USRDS columns alone.
