### PMC9613499

[Trends, Social Context, and Transplant Implications of Obesity Among Incident Dialysis Patients in the United States.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9613499/) · 2022 · doi:10.1097/tp.0000000000004243

Reading: **MT**, 2026-11-15. Main-text reading; figures as images and supplements not inspected; tables not comprehensively checked unless stated.

- **Role / population / unit:** incident US dialysis adults 2007–2016; person/BMI trend and separate under-75 waitlisting cohort. [reported Methods]
- **Sources:** USRDS baseline height/weight, CMS-2728 comorbidities, UNOS-linked dates and ACS 2011–2015 Social Deprivation Index. Geographic analysis restricted to matching initiation years. [reported Methods]
- **Data needs:** exclude preexisting waitlisting; substitute transplant date when no prior listing date exists. Fine–Gray models account for death over one year. Entirely blank comorbidity section without explicit “none” is missing, not disease-free. [reported Methods]
- **Pitfalls:** surrogate listing dates need flags. BMI at dialysis start is not a trajectory; fluid/body composition and missingness matter. Separate broad trend denominator from complete-case waitlisting denominator. Area deprivation is not individual income. [reported Methods; Limitations; inference]
- **Locators:** full main text; tables/supplements/images unread. This supplies a concrete missing-versus-none convention, but the exact form/release mapping must be verified before package adoption.
