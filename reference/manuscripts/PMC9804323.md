### PMC9804323

[Safety and effectiveness of apixaban versus warfarin for acute venous thromboembolism in patients with end-stage kidney disease: A national cohort study.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9804323/) · 2022 · doi:10.1002/jhm.12926

Reading: **MT**, 2026-11-15. Main-text reading; figures as images and supplements not inspected; tables not comprehensively checked unless stated.

- **Role / population / unit:** new-user active-comparator study of apixaban versus warfarin for VTE, dialysis patients 2014–June 2018; person, hospitalization and follow-up. [reported Methods]
- **Sources:** institutional, carrier, MedPAR, outpatient and Part D claims; six-month continuous primary fee-for-service A/B/D. VTE diagnosis allowed 30 days before through seven days after first fill. [reported Methods]
- **Data needs:** exclude recent anticoagulant use and AF/hospice; recurrent VTE requires primary-diagnosis hospitalization beginning >21 days after index fill. Count only the most severe bleed per admission. Primary initial-treatment analysis uses truncated IPTW and competing-death models; discontinuation-gap censoring is separate. [reported Methods]
- **Pitfalls:** tablet strength cannot establish loading dose, and inpatient anticoagulation is unobserved. Early recurrence during a prolonged index stay is omitted. Future diagnosis confirmation and dose-subgroup classification require explicit temporal handling; treatment initiation year matters because uptake changed rapidly. [reported Methods; Limitations; inference]
