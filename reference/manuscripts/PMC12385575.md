[Association of Therapeutic Plasma Exchange-Treated Thrombotic Thrombocytopenic Purpura with Improved Mortality Outcome in End-Stage Renal Disease.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12385575/) · 2025 · doi:10.3390/diseases13080247

- **Role / population / unit:** dialysis initiators 2005–2018; person with claims-defined TPE-treated thrombotic microangiopathy and mortality from dialysis start. [reported Methods]
- **Sources:** CMS-2728 and hospital/detailed/physician claims. Exposure requires diagnosis plus plasma exchange within seven days. [reported Methods]
- **Data needs:** preserve diagnosis and procedure dates, their interval and treatment-conditioned case definition; untreated disease is not identified by this algorithm. [reported Methods]
- **Pitfalls:** if treatment occurs after dialysis entry, baseline ever-treated classification can create immortal time; propensity weighting does not fix this. Very large protective associations require temporal/phenotype audit before interpretation. Claimed new TTP code D69.3 is suspect and must be checked against official codebooks. Results' mean survival unit differs from the Methods' years. [reported Methods; Results; Discussion; inference]
- **Locators:** complete main text; code tables/supplements unread. No ADAMTS13 confirmation is available. This is a cautionary phenotype/timing reference, not evidence that adding a TTP diagnosis or TPE protects against all-cause mortality.
