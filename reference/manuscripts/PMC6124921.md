### PMC6124921

[The Lifetime Health Burden of Delayed Graft Function in Kidney Transplant Recipients in the United States.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6124921/) · 2018 · doi:10.1177/2381468318781811

Reading: **MT**, 2026-11-15. Main-text reading; figures as images and supplements not inspected; tables not comprehensively checked unless stated.

- **Role / population / unit:** USRDS adult kidney transplants 2001–2014; person with repeated transplant/graft-failure transitions and simulated lifetime trajectories. [reported Methods]
- **Sources:** registry transplant/outcome history plus external quality-of-life weights. Exact field and DGF extraction mappings are not established in main text. [reported Methods]
- **Data needs:** semi-Markov clock resets on state entry; separate functioning-graft, dialysis and death transitions. Fit non-DGF hazards, then apply adjusted DGF hazard ratios. Update age and retransplant status at transitions; sample subsequent DGF conditionally. [reported Methods]
- **Pitfalls:** observed 13.7-year follow-up differs from extrapolated lifetime. Parametric hazard choice, constant extrapolated effects and utility weights are assumptions; probabilistic sensitivity is not observed validation. Do not collapse multiple transplants to earliest patient date. [reported Methods; Limitations; inference]
- **Locators:** full main text; parameter tables, algorithms and supplements unread. Useful for row-varying baselines/state histories; it does not establish a causal DGF effect or approved package coefficients.
