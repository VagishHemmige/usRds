### PMC6815249

[Transfers to Hemodialysis Among US Patients Initiating Renal Replacement Therapy With Peritoneal Dialysis.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6815249/) · 2019 · doi:10.1053/j.ajkd.2019.05.014

Reading: **MT**, 2026-11-15. Main-text reading; figures as images and supplements not inspected; tables not comprehensively checked unless stated.

- **Role / population / unit:** adults initiating PD as first modality in 2008–2011; first sustained HD transfer with competing death/transplant, followed through July 2015. [reported Methods]
- **Sources:** Treatment History, CMS-2728/Patients and Medicare claims for first peritonitis. No explicit continuous Medicare eligibility rule is stated for that claims-derived covariate. [reported Methods; inference]
- **Data needs:** disregard HD/transplant episodes <90 days if PD resumes; deaths within 30 days of last PD count without transfer. Baseline Fine–Gray model yields risk-score quartiles; separate cause-specific model adds time-varying peritonitis. Albumin mean imputation follows sensitivity analyses. [reported Methods]
- **Pitfalls:** these sustained-transfer rules answer a different question from one-day transfer definitions. Peritonitis codes can represent suspected/other abdominal disease and lack microbiology. Risk-score discrimination is not external validation; changing model type changes the estimand. [reported Discussion; inference]
- **Source checks:** 12,121/29,573 is about 41%, not the reported 28.5%; five-year PD continuation is described inconsistently. [reported Results; Discussion]
