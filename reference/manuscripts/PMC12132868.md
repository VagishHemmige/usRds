[Nationwide Trends in Stroke Among Patients Undergoing Hemodialysis by Sex and Race: An Analysis From the US Renal Database.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12132868/) · 2025 · doi:10.1161/jaha.124.036468

Reading: **MT**, 2026-11-15. Main-text reading; figures as images and supplements not inspected; tables not comprehensively checked unless stated.

- **Role / population / unit:** 391,195 incident HD patients, 2006–2016, surviving a 90-day eligibility period; first follow-up stroke hospitalization. [reported Methods; Results]
- **Sources:** CMS-2728 and A/B claims, followed through 2019; D coverage not required. [reported Methods]
- **Data needs:** separate ischemic/hemorrhagic code families and age-standardize to the 2006 ESRD population. Censor coverage loss, transplant and death. Prior cerebrovascular disease remains eligible, so “first” is during follow-up, not lifetime onset. [reported Methods; inference]
- **Pitfalls:** AF ascertainment through six months extends beyond day-90 entry; requires timing clarification to avoid future information. Three-digit ICD-9 families need validated subcode rules before reuse. Kaplan–Meier with competing deaths censored does not estimate real-world cumulative incidence. One-/three-year horizons need explicit anchoring to initiation versus entry. [reported Methods; Discussion; inference]
