- **Data needs:** exclude prior-six-month PIM users and people dying or leaving eligible treatment/coverage within 90 days. Construct monthly exposures from fills and days supplied with a seven-day gap allowance; time-varying Cox models. Random patient-level development/validation split prevents repeated rows crossing samples. [reported Methods; inference]
- **Pitfalls:** this survivor-selected cohort is not all incident HD. Classes are labeled higher/lower risk by the hazard-ratio point estimate, irrespective of confidence interval; these are observational categories, not prescribing recommendations. Clarify inclusive dates, grace periods and the 90-day eligibility landmark before coding. Confounding by indication remains. [reported Methods; Discussion; inference]
