### PMC10336615

[The Association of Dialysis Facility Payer Mix With Access to Kidney Transplantation.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10336615/) · 2023 · doi:10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2023.22803

Reading: **MT**, 2026-11-15. Main-text reading; figures as images and supplements not inspected; tables not comprehensively checked unless stated.

- **Role / population / unit:** adults aged 18–75 initiating dialysis in 2013–2017; person clustered within facility, with facility commercial payer mix exposure. [reported Methods]
- **Sources:** CMS-2728 insurance/clinical information, USRDS waitlist, Dialysis Facility Compare and county 2016 Social Vulnerability Index. Exclude prior transplant and selected contraindications. [reported Methods]
- **Data needs:** any commercial coverage overrides other reported coverage; Medicare Advantage is classified noncommercial. Aggregate incident patients' initial insurance over the study period, not current prevalent-facility payer mix. Sequential Cox adjustment separates patient insurance from facility exposure, with clustered variance. [reported Methods]
- **Pitfalls:** whole-period facility summaries incorporate later entrants; suitability as a baseline predictor requires temporal review. Insurance switching is unmeasured. Waitlisting is not referral, and reporting death censoring “within one year of listing” is ambiguous for a dialysis-to-listing endpoint. Clarify preemptive-listing and transfer handling before reproduction. [reported Methods; Limitations; inference]
- **Locators:** complete main text; tables/images/supplements unread. The study-specific insurance hierarchy and transplant exclusions are not general eligibility rules.
