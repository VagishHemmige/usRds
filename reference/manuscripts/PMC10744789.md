### PMC10744789

[Atopic Dermatitis and the Risk of Infection in End-Stage Renal Disease.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10744789/) · 2023 · doi:10.3390/medicina59122145

Reading: **MT**, 2026-11-15. Main-text reading; figures as images and supplements not inspected; tables not comprehensively checked unless stated.

- **Role / population / unit:** 2004–2019 dialysis initiators; person, postdialysis atopic-dermatitis diagnosis and first infection of each type. [reported Methods]
- **Sources:** CMS-2728, patient file, hospital/detailed/physician claims; ICD-9/10 definitions in supplement. [reported Methods]
- **Data needs:** AD must precede the infection; infection-specific follow-up ends at first diagnosis, death or end-2019. Distinguish recorded diagnosis from clinical prevalence. [reported Methods]
- **Pitfalls:** exposure identified after dialysis cannot safely be assigned from dialysis start without handling time before diagnosis. A log-person-time offset in a logistic model does not by itself turn odds ratios into rate ratios or risk ratios. Continuous claims eligibility is not described; missing access exclusion leaves an almost entirely HD cohort. Reported sample totals differ by four. [reported Methods; Results; inference]
- **Locators:** full main text; tables/supplement unread. Useful as a phenotype-design example and cautionary comparator, not a validated infection algorithm or causal estimate. Treatment and severity are not measured.
