### PMC10870477

[Mortality risk in patients with autosomal dominant polycystic kidney disease.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10870477/) · 2024 · doi:10.1186/s12882-024-03484-3

Reading: **MT**, 2026-11-15. Main-text reading; figures as images and supplements not inspected; tables not comprehensively checked unless stated.

- **Role / population / unit:** 2014–2016 ADPKD mortality in separate non-ESRD CKD and ESRD cohorts; person and age-split interval. [reported Methods]
- **Sources:** Medicare 5% CKD sample versus comprehensive ESRD registry, with claims-defined CKD stage. [reported Methods]
- **Data needs:** delayed entry at later study start/diagnosis or ESRD service; non-ESRD follow-up ends at ESRD onset. Split person-time at age boundaries for older-adult rates; records then exceed unique people. Standardize to a named external age distribution. [reported Methods]
- **Pitfalls:** transplant takes precedence over prior dialysis at baseline, not necessarily current functioning graft. Different sampling frames and eligibility make crude mortality comparisons misleading. Missing/zero-event categories need separate handling; Firth estimation addresses separation but not selection bias. [reported Methods; Discussion; inference]
- **Locators:** complete main text; phenotype table/supplements unread. Do not sum repeated age-split records as patients, infer laboratory CKD stage from diagnosis codes, or interpret the paper's data-access wording as permission to redistribute registry data.
