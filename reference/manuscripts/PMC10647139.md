### PMC10647139

[Associations between abrupt transition, dialysis-requiring AKI, and early mortality in ESKD among U.S. veterans.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10647139/) · 2023 · doi:10.1186/s12882-023-03387-9

Reading: **MT**, 2026-11-15. Main-text reading; figures as images and supplements not inspected; tables not comprehensively checked unless stated.

- **Role / population / unit:** VA-linked incident HD patients, 2009–2013; individual transition pattern and 90-day mortality. [reported Methods]
- **Sources:** VA outpatient/inpatient creatinine and encounters linked to USRDS ESRD onset/access and death data. [reported Methods]
- **Data needs:** abrupt transition uses mean outpatient eGFR in days −365 to −7. AKI-D requires hospitalized dialysis plus creatinine rise, with ESRD onset during admission or within 30 days after discharge. Separate absolute-only from ≥50% creatinine rises. [reported Methods]
- **Pitfalls:** these laboratory phenotypes cannot be recreated from USRDS diagnosis codes alone. Excluding patients without outpatient labs selects VA users; inpatient deaths before registry enrollment may be missed. Nephrology visits/access can be mediators rather than baseline confounders. [reported Methods; Discussion; inference]
- **Locators:** full main text; tables/figures unread. Keep baseline-to-hospital and baseline-to-ESRD windows distinct. The first 90 days are the outcome window here, so a routine day-91 cohort entry would discard the scientific target.
