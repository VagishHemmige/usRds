- **Role / population / unit:** nondiabetic kidney recipients, 2005–2011; person and posttransplant diabetes diagnosis. [reported Methods]
- **Sources:** USRDS, UNOS, Medicare claims and revised CMS-2728 HbA1c; exclude diabetes across multiple sources and extreme HbA1c. [reported Methods]
- **Data needs:** retain measurement-to-transplant interval; first posttransplant 249/250 claim defines onset. Censor death/graft failure; separate 45-day and one-year landmark analyses. Center-clustered Cox models explore spline thresholds. [reported Methods]
- **Pitfalls:** only a small selected subset has HbA1c and complete covariates; claims availability is not continuous coverage. Data-derived 5.4% threshold is not externally validated.
