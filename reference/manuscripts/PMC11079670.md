### PMC11079670

[A clinical score to predict recovery in end-stage kidney disease due to acute kidney injury.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11079670/) · 2024 · doi:10.1093/ckj/sfae085

Reading: **MT**, 2026-11-15. Main-text reading; figures as images and supplements not inspected; tables not comprehensively checked unless stated.

- **Role / population / unit:** 22,922 adults with provider-attributed tubular necrosis starting dialysis in 2005–2014; person and recovery by 90 days/12 months. [reported Methods]
- **Sources:** PATIENTS, MEDEVID and RXHIST. Study codes 5836/5836A are attributed-cause values, not a generic claims phenotype. [reported Methods]
- **Data needs:** death/transplant before recovery counts as nonrecovery at each horizon. Separate logistic models produce rounded integer scores; jackknife and bootstrap assess internal optimism. [reported Methods]
- **Pitfalls:** excludes missing selected demographics, access and labs; “unknown nephrology care” remains a predictor distinct from no care. ATN attribution does not encompass all AKI. Internal discrimination is not external validation, calibration or evidence for a race-based clinical default. Exact recovery episode/duration algorithm is insufficiently specified in main text. [reported Methods; Discussion; inference]
- **Locators:** complete main text; score tables/figures unread. Do not implement coefficients or recovery labels from narrative alone; verify RXHIST coding and the full scoring table before any approved implementation.
