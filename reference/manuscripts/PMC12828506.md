### PMC12828506

[Regional Variation in Early Kidney Transplant Access Across Dialysis Facilities in 4 US Regions.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12828506/) · 2026 · doi:10.1016/j.ekir.2025.103721

Reading: **MT**, 2026-11-15. Main-text reading; figures as images and supplements not inspected; tables not comprehensively checked unless stated.

- **Role / population / unit:** 62,467 referred and 27,171 evaluated adults, 2015–2023; dialysis-facility proportions across four US regions. [reported Methods]
- **Sources:** 36/47 participating E-STAR transplant centers, USRDS/CMS-2728/facility data, linked SRTR waitlisting and 2014–2018 ACS. [reported Methods]
- **Data needs:** evaluation within six months of first referral; active or inactive listing within one year of evaluation. Attribute to first dialysis facility; exclude preemptive pathways. Insurance hierarchy places employer coverage, Medicare, then Medicaid—different from related access papers. [reported Methods]
- **Pitfalls:** incomplete center participation prevents an all-dialysis referral denominator. Methods defines waitlisting among evaluated, but Discussion says referred; follow-up/calendar wording and large exclusion counts require flowchart/supplement verification. Facility medians and proportions are not patient-weighted regional probabilities; small denominators can produce 0%/100% extremes. [reported Methods; Discussion; inference]
- **Locators:** complete main prose; tables/flowchart/supplements unread. Center-specific evaluation definitions and data-end dates matter. Keep referral, evaluation and listing cohorts separate and retain coverage/attribution metadata rather than presenting these rates as national access estimates.
