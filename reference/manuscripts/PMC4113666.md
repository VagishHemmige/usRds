### PMC4113666

[Confounders of mortality and hospitalization rate calculations for profit and nonprofit dialysis facilities: analytic augmentation.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4113666/) · 2014 · doi:10.1186/1471-2369-15-121

Reading: **MT**, 2026-11-15. Main-text reading; figures as images and supplements not inspected; tables not comprehensively checked unless stated.

- **Role / population / unit:** USRDS provider-level comparison of for-profit/nonprofit dialysis facilities in 2010; patient-time and facility ownership. [reported Methods]
- **Files and fields:** institutional Part A claims, core demographic/clinical information and successive 2007–2010 Facility Reports; exact field names not given. [reported Methods]
- **Data needs:** Medicare Part A primary; time starts January 1 or later ESRD entry and ends at death, dialysis discontinuation or year-end. Rates use cumulative time; negative-binomial models address overdispersion. Add network, ownership duration, initial access and predialysis care sequentially. [reported Methods]
- **Pitfalls:** missing access/care excludes different samples across models; imputation is a separate sensitivity analysis. Ownership must be dated rather than taken from a current directory. Insignificant adjusted differences do not establish equivalence or remove unmeasured confounding. Person/facility attribution after transfers and hospital-episode construction need further specification. [reported Methods; Discussion; inference]
- **Locators:** complete main text; tables/images unread. This is a case-mix modeling example, not a universal provider-ranking adjustment rule.
