### PMC10907919

[Foot and Ankle Care by Podiatrists and Amputations in Patients With Diabetes and Kidney Failure.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10907919/) · 2024 · doi:10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2024.0801

Reading: **MT**, 2026-11-15. Main-text reading; figures as images and supplements not inspected; tables not comprehensively checked unless stated.

- **Role / population / unit:** USRDS adults with diabetes receiving dialysis and first observed diabetic foot ulcer in 2017; person indexed at ulcer diagnosis. [reported Methods]
- **Sources:** Medicare claims, provider specialty and procedure codes, CMS-2728; exclude prior-year ulcer, prior major amputation, transplant and incomplete baseline data. [reported Methods]
- **Data needs:** podiatry exposure in preceding three months; Cox composite death/major-amputation and competing-death subdistribution model for amputation alone. Follow through 2019; the study defines loss by 60 consecutive days without claims. [reported Methods]
- **Pitfalls:** a claims gap is not verified coverage loss. Age/function from ESRD onset can predate ulcer baseline by years. Selection on developing an ulcer changes the estimand from population-wide prevention. The main-text code list includes G02460; validate against year-specific official coding and the supplement before implementation, without silently correcting it. [reported Methods; inference]
- **Locators:** complete main text; supplements and tables unread. Negative-control and alternative-exposure analyses probe robustness but do not eliminate residual confounding or prove a causal podiatry benefit.
