### PMC9091783

[Mortality Trends After Transfer From Peritoneal Dialysis to Hemodialysis.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9091783/) · 2022 · doi:10.1016/j.ekir.2022.02.016

Reading: **MT**, 2026-11-15. Main-text reading; figures as images and supplements not inspected; tables not comprehensively checked unless stated.

- **Role / population / unit:** four-registry comparative analysis including USRDS; adults starting PD within 180 days of KRT, 2000–2014 (Canada through 2013); first PD-to-HD transfer per person. [reported Study Population]
- **Sources / timing:** registry modality sequences, death/transplant and baseline covariates; HD for ≥1 day qualifies, with no 30/90-day survival requirement. [reported Methods]
- **Data needs:** follow from first HD day regardless of later return to PD; censor at transplant, loss or end-2015 (Canada end-2014). Analyze early/medium/late periods separately and combine registry estimates by meta-analysis. [reported Methods]
- **Pitfalls:** attributing the first 30–90 days to the old modality would conceal the studied early-transfer risk. One-day transfers may be under-recorded during hospitalizations; death-cause completeness differs by registry. Most comorbidities and transfer reasons are unavailable consistently. This comparison cannot identify the causal effect of transferring. [reported Discussion]
