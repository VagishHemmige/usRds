### PMC7733722

[Renal deposits of complement factors as predictors of end-stage renal disease and death in patients with lupus nephritis.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7733722/) · 2020 · doi:10.1093/rheumatology/keaa174

Reading: **MT**, 2026-11-15. Main-text reading; figures as images and supplements not inspected; tables not comprehensively checked unless stated.

- **Role / population / unit:** external lupus-nephritis biopsy cohort linked to USRDS for incident ESRD; adult first native biopsy, 1990–2016; person. [reported Methods]
- **Sources:** local registry/records and kidney pathology provide exposures/covariates; USRDS supplies ESRD dates, National Death Index supplies death dates through December 2016. Linkage occurs under a data-use agreement. [reported Methods]
- **Data needs:** biopsy is the clinical baseline; composite first ESRD/death, separate mortality Cox model and ESRD Fine–Gray model with death competing. Adjustment excludes patients missing relevant covariates. [reported Methods]
- **Pitfalls:** biopsy complement staining and clinical laboratory values are external measurements, not USRDS columns. The one-year eGFR analysis excludes ESRD cases and requires available measurements, producing a selected subgroup. Prebiopsy medication ascertainment does not measure postbiopsy treatment. Linkage performance and exact registry fields are unreported. [reported Methods; Discussion; inference]
- **Locators:** complete main text; tables/supplements/images unread. Useful for separating linked-registry outcome ascertainment from claims-defined disease cohorts; it does not validate a claims algorithm for lupus nephritis.
