- **Role / population / unit:** Southern Community Cohort Study case-cohort sample; individual baseline specimen and incident treated kidney failure through March 2015. [reported Methods]
- **Sources:** external interviews/creatinine linked to USRDS, not USRDS laboratory fields. Exclude registry ESRD preceding cohort enrollment. [reported Methods]
- **Data needs:** inverse sampling-probability weights reconstruct the blood-donor subcohort; weighted case-cohort Cox differs from the multistate analysis, which omits extra-subcohort cases. Time begins at enrollment; ESRD and death are alternative first events. [reported Methods]
- **Pitfalls:** one baseline creatinine cannot estimate slope. Restricted splines and race interaction are study modeling choices; historical CKD-EPI calculation is not a recommendation for current race-based estimation. Albuminuria is unavailable. [reported Methods; Discussion; inference]
- **Locators:** complete prose main text; tables/figures/supplements unread. Do not estimate absolute cumulative incidence by simply censoring competing deaths, or combine enriched case counts with an unweighted population denominator.
