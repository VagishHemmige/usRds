### PMC4731835

[Attribution of cause of end-stage renal disease among patients with systemic lupus erythematosus: the Georgia Lupus Registry.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4731835/) · 2016 · doi:10.1136/lupus-2015-000132

Reading: **MT**, 2026-11-15. Main-text reading; figures as images and supplements not inspected; tables not comprehensively checked unless stated.

- **Role / population / unit:** 251 Georgia Lupus Registry cases with subsequent ESRD; person and provider-attributed cause versus clinically ascertained SLE. [reported Methods]
- **Sources:** medical-record registry, USRDS CMS-2728 cause, later inpatient claims, Census tract context. Single cause code 710.0 identifies SLE attribution. [reported Methods]
- **Data needs:** preserve clinical SLE, lupus nephritis and attributed kidney-failure cause as separate concepts. Additional inpatient diagnoses increase capture among those hospitalized, with a different denominator. Missing causes are excluded here. [reported Methods; Results]
- **Pitfalls:** 79% capture is not specificity or positive predictive value, nor proof that all other causes are erroneous. Future hospitalizations cannot serve as baseline information without temporal leakage. Day 90 is not universal proof of observable Medicare claims. Linkage description and historical cohort dates need reconciliation before replication. [reported Discussion; inference]
- **Locators:** complete main text; tables/images unread. One cause field cannot represent multimorbidity; differential attribution by age can bias cohort comparisons even when coding is implemented correctly.
