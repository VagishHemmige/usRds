[Hemodialysis Vascular Access and Risk of Major Bleeding, Thrombosis, and Cardiovascular Events: A Cohort Study.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9189779/) · 2022 · doi:10.1016/j.xkme.2022.100456

Reading: **MT**, 2026-11-15. Main-text reading; figures as images and supplements not inspected; tables not comprehensively checked unless stated.

- **Role / population / unit:** new functioning AV fistula/graft users on HD, USRDS 2010–2015; person/access interval and repeated bleeding event. [reported Methods]
- **Sources:** CMS-2728/2746, Treatment History, core and A/B/D claims; continuous Medicare-primary and D from kidney-failure incidence to first AV use. Exclude recent oral anticoagulants. [reported Methods]
- **Data needs:** outpatient modifiers V6/V7 identify graft/fistula only without V5; any accompanying V5 indicates catheter. Allow temporary catheter use ≤30 days. Baseline access defines exposure; censor prolonged catheter/PD, anticoagulation initiation, transplant, coverage loss, death, three years or August 2015. [reported Methods]
- **Pitfalls:** functioning-access selection excludes maturation failures; exposure begins at use, not surgery. Recurrent bleeding allows one event/day, while other endpoints stop at first event. One-year covariate lookback may extend before required coverage for early access users. [reported Methods; Limitations; inference]
