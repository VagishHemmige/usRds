### PMC12421340

[Left Atrial Appendage Occlusion vs Anticoagulants in Dialysis With Atrial Fibrillation.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12421340/) · 2025 · doi:10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2025.30990

Reading: **MT**, 2026-11-15. Main-text reading; figures as images and supplements not inspected; tables not comprehensively checked unless stated.

- **Role / population / unit:** dialysis patients with newly identified atrial fibrillation in USRDS 2015–2018; person/time-varying anticoagulant or left atrial appendage occlusion exposure and recurrent bleeding. [reported Methods]
- **Sources:** A/B/D claims, CMS-2728, procedure codes and registry death data; six-month coverage/lookback, prior anticoagulant exclusions and six-month survival from first service. [reported Methods]
- **Data needs:** AF requires repeated outpatient or one hospital claim; patients may switch from anticoagulant to device exposure. Joint frailty analysis models recurrent bleeding and terminal death, with nonfatal stroke time-updated. [reported Methods]
- **Pitfalls:** six-week postdevice bleeding is excluded, so this is not total procedural-plus-long-term bleeding risk. Crude Kaplan–Meier curves explicitly ignore exposure changes. AF diagnosis versus treatment-start time origins and untreated intervals need reconciliation. Matching on treatment year and risk score does not remove residual confounding. [reported Methods; Limitations; inference]
- **Locators:** complete main text; supplements/tables/images unread. Results alternates 2051 and 2053 anticoagulant-only patients within a stated 2344 total. Joint-event association does not alone establish mediation of survival benefit through fewer bleeds.
