### PMC12008760

[Calcimimetic Prescriptions in Fee-for-Service Medicare Beneficiaries Undergoing Dialysis.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12008760/) · 2025 · doi:10.1001/jamahealthforum.2025.0452

Reading: **MT**, 2026-11-15. Main-text reading; figures as images and supplements not inspected; tables not comprehensively checked unless stated.

- **Role / population / unit:** 509,765 FFS dialysis beneficiaries, 2016–2020; 4,435,058 patient-quarters. [reported Methods; Results]
- **Sources:** USRDS A/B/D, CROWNWeb, facility survey and Dialysis Facility Compare. Each contributing quarter requires one year of consecutive FFS coverage. [reported Methods]
- **Data needs:** calcimimetics move from Part D before 2018 to Part B dialysis codes J0604/J0606 in 2018–2020. Quarterly majority subsidy category defines exposure; two-way fixed-effects difference-in-differences compares subsidy groups, clustering by facility. [reported Methods]
- **Pitfalls:** prepolicy non-Part-D use is unobserved, not truly zero; its estimated increase is an upper bound. Postpolicy out-of-pocket costs are unavailable. Pretrend tests are statistically significant despite small magnitudes. Covariate prose first lists dual eligibility then says it was excluded for collinearity. [reported Methods; Discussion]
