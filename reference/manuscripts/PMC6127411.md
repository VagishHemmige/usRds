### PMC6127411

[Mortality in Living Kidney Donors With ESRD: A Propensity Score Analysis Using the United States Renal Data System.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6127411/) · 2018 · doi:10.1016/j.ekir.2018.04.005

Reading: **MT**, 2026-11-15. Main-text reading; figures as images and supplements not inspected; tables not comprehensively checked unless stated.

- **Role / population / unit:** USRDS propensity-matched survival analysis of prior living kidney donors versus nondonors starting hemodialysis, 1995–2009; person and matched pair. [reported Methods]
- **Files and fields:** Medicare claims donor code, CMS-2728 demographic/comorbidity data, dialysis/transplant/death information; exact SAF mappings unreported. [reported Methods; Limitations]
- **Data needs:** Medicare primary at dialysis initiation; exclude age <18 and peritoneal dialysis. One-to-one matching without replacement on transformed propensity score; retain pairs for shared-frailty survival models and split follow-up into 0–5, 5–10 and >10 years. [reported Methods]
- **Pitfalls:** baseline payer status does not specify full donor-history ascertainment. Transplant rates differ substantially; the mortality censoring/adjustment contract needs explicit reconstruction. Albumin/BMI missingness changes sensitivity-analysis samples. Matched nondonors are not representative of all dialysis patients, and this comparison cannot estimate donation's causal effect in healthy candidates. [reported Results; Limitations; inference]
