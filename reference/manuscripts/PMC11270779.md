### PMC11270779

[Setting reasonable goals for kidney transplant referral among dialysis facilities.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11270779/) · 2024 · doi:10.1186/s12882-024-03671-2

Reading: **MT**, 2026-11-15. Main-text reading; figures as images and supplements not inspected; tables not comprehensively checked unless stated.

- **Role / population / unit:** adults starting dialysis in GA/NC/SC, 2012–2017; person linked to regional E-STAR referral records. Preemptive referrals/listings included, preemptive transplant excluded. [reported Methods]
- **Sources:** CMS-2728 clinical predictors, external referral dates and USRDS waitlist dates. Regional referral capture is not a standard national USRDS field. [reported Methods]
- **Data needs:** referral within one year of dialysis; waitlisting within two years of referral; preemptive events assigned one day. Logistic predicted waitlisting probability developed among referred patients and applied to nonreferred patients, using a 0.34 cutoff. [reported Methods; Results]
- **Pitfalls:** “propensity score” predicts an outcome, not treatment assignment. Waitlisting is a proxy for suitability influenced by access barriers; AUROC and same-sample cutoff do not establish external clinical validity. Sensitivity changes both training cohort and outcome horizon. Follow-up is described as December 2020 versus March 13, 2020; reconcile dates. [reported Methods; Discussion; inference]
- **Locators:** complete main text; tables/supplements/images unread. Do not install the proposed referral target or score as a validated national quality benchmark.
