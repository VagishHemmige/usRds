- **Role / population / unit:** 2009 incident kidney-failure cohort with ten-year histories; person-month state and compressed successive-state sequence. [reported Methods]
- **Sources:** RXHIST60 and CMS-2728; six states include in-center/home dialysis, deceased/living transplant, stopped dialysis and death. [reported Methods]
- **Data needs:** RXHIST60 already filters short treatment changes. Within a month select longest-duration treatment, breaking ties by earliest modality. Compress repeated states only for sequence order; keep durations separately. [reported Methods]
- **Pitfalls:** recovery and loss-to-follow-up exclusions use future information and affect group composition. Stopped dialysis is not recovery or death. Monthly state prevalence, ever-treated proportions and transition frequencies have different denominators. Full-registry sampling does not remove measurement/selection uncertainty.
