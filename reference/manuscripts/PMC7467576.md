- **Role / population / unit:** USRDS inpatient infection ascertainment; dialysis initiation 2004–2010, ages 11–100; person and first postdialysis syphilis diagnosis, with annual estimates through 2011. [reported Study cohort; Results]
- **Files and fields:** hospital diagnoses/procedures, patient demographics and CMS-2728; exact SAF mappings unreported. Neurosyphilis requires a diagnosis plus lumbar puncture 90 days before to 30 days after. [reported Outcome variables]
- **Data needs:** exclude prior syphilis and people without hospital claims; 5% sample of noncases for risk-factor analysis; control covariates queried through 604 days after dialysis. [reported Methods]
- **Pitfalls:** procedure evidence is not confirmatory CSF testing; outpatient-only cases are missed. Printed results mix patient and person-year denominators: the eight-year value numerically equals cases divided by cohort size, requiring reconciliation before reuse. Age/ethnicity directions also differ across Results and Discussion. [reported Limitations; unclear Results versus Discussion; inference]
