### PMC10719652

[Sex/Gender-Based Disparities in Early Transplant Access by Attributed Cause of Kidney Disease-Evidence from a Multiregional Cohort in the Southeast United States.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10719652/) · 2023 · doi:10.1016/j.ekir.2023.09.010

Reading: **MT**, 2026-11-15. Main-text reading; figures as images and supplements not inspected; tables not comprehensively checked unless stated.

- **Role / population / unit:** 43,548 incident dialysis adults aged 18–<80 in GA/NC/SC, 2015–2019; sex/gender differences by attributed kidney-failure cause. [reported Methods]
- **Sources:** USRDS/CMS-2728 and facility data linked to nine-center E-STAR referral/evaluation records and ACS ZIP characteristics. [reported Methods]
- **Data needs:** referral within twelve months among incident patients; evaluation within six months among referred; waitlisting among evaluated and separately among all incident patients. Preemptive referral/listing receives one day. Insurance hierarchy is private, Medicaid, Medicare, other. [reported Methods]
- **Pitfalls:** administrative binary sex does not measure self-identified gender. Primary crude models and explanatory adjusted models answer different questions; the authors' causal interpretation is not a general adjustment rule. Referral/evaluation end December 2020 but waitlisting ends November 13. Results says December 2015 initiation where Methods says January; verify before replication. [reported Methods; Results; inference]
- **Locators:** Study Population, Outcomes, Covariates, Statistical Analysis and complete Discussion; tables/supplements unread. Keep stage-specific origins, denominators and preemptive flags explicit; linked prewaitlisting data are not ordinary USRDS fields.
