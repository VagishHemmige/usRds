### PMC13098409

[Gabapentin Utilization and Adverse Effects Among US Hemodialysis Patients Diagnosed With Pruritus or Neuropathic Pain.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13098409/) · 2026 · doi:10.1016/j.xkme.2026.101341

Reading: **MT**, 2026-11-15. Main-text reading; figures as images and supplements not inspected; tables not comprehensively checked unless stated.

- **Role / population / unit:** USRDS HD patients 2016–2020, fee-for-service primary payer and ≥2 months Part D; daily dose/diagnosis states and recurrent adverse events. [reported Methods]
- **Sources:** Part D quantity, strength and days supplied; MBSF demographics, CMS-2728 and diagnosis claims. Exclude first-month gabapentinoid users. [reported Methods]
- **Data needs:** dose = quantity × strength / days supplied, then study-specific rounding/exclusions. Diagnosis states accumulate without expiration. Andersen–Gill models use time-updated dose, diagnosis strata, prior-event counts and person-clustered variance; events have a 90-day moratorium. [reported Methods]
- **Pitfalls:** clarify whether moratorium removes person-time as well as events, overlap/refill rules, and coverage-end censoring. One-month observed washout does not establish lifetime new use. Diagnoses are imperfect indication proxies. Results reports 57% receiving ≥100 mg pregabalin but 85% ≥200 mg; this nesting is impossible without different denominators.
