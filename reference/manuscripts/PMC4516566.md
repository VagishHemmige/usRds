### PMC4516566

[Kidney Failure and ESRD in the Atherosclerosis Risk in Communities (ARIC) Study: Comparing Ascertainment of Treated and Untreated Kidney Failure in a Cohort Study.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4516566/) · 2015 · doi:10.1053/j.ajkd.2015.01.016

Reading: **MT**, 2026-11-15. Main-text reading; figures as images and supplements not inspected; tables not comprehensively checked unless stated.

- **Role / population / unit:** ARIC linkage and validation of USRDS-treated ESRD versus broader treated/untreated kidney failure; person and sampled hospitalization. [reported Methods]
- **Sources / timing:** ARIC visits, hospitalization/death codes, physician chart review and USRDS linkage; visit 4 baseline 1996–1998; validation through 2008, other analyses through 2010. [reported Methods]
- **Data needs:** separate reference standards for permanent treated failure and all kidney failure. Stratified chart sampling oversamples dialysis/transplant; validation weights use inverse sampling probability. [reported Chart Review; Statistical Analysis]
- **Pitfalls:** USRDS membership is not a complete untreated-kidney-failure definition. Early deaths and delayed registration explain some discordance. Though the proposed definition includes study-visit eGFR <15, no incident events were identified that way during this follow-up. Recovery information was unavailable; acute versus permanent dialysis can be misclassified. [reported Results; Discussion]
- **Code locator:** the hierarchical code algorithm is in the main-text Figure 1 legend, inspected as text: USRDS ESRD, kidney-failure death, kidney-failure hospitalization, dialysis hospitalization, then transplant hospitalization. The legend includes both diagnosis and procedure codes; preserve their code system, setting and hierarchy before extraction. [reported Figure 1 legend]
