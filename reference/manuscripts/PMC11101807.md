Reading: **MT**, 2026-11-15. Main-text reading; figures as images and supplements not inspected; tables not comprehensively checked unless stated.

- **Role / population / unit:** women aged 15–49 on home dialysis, 2005–2018; repeated eligible intervals and reconstructed pregnancy episodes. [reported Methods]
- **Sources:** claims, RXHIST, CMS-2728, residence file, ACS/RUCA. Pregnancy endpoints and outcome-specific gestational lengths determine imputed conception dates. [reported Methods]
- **Data needs:** claims hierarchy deduplicates episodes; overlap rules and minimum spacing distinguish repeat pregnancies. Age, vintage and modality vary over time; PWP total-time models stratify first versus subsequent events. [reported Methods]
- **Pitfalls:** 40-week future survival/coverage requirement selects observable pregnancies. This confirmation window can extend beyond home dialysis or transplantation, whereas at-risk time cannot. Short home-HD gaps are bridged under a separate <30-day rule. Unknown fetal outcome remains unknown, not loss or live birth. [reported Methods; Discussion; inference]
- **Locators:** full main text; code supplement/figures unread. Pregnancy rates count episodes rather than women, and imputed conception uncertainty affects assignment of modality at conception.
