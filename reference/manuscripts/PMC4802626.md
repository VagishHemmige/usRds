### PMC4802626

[Comparative effectiveness of home dialysis therapies: a matched cohort study.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4802626/) · 2016 · doi:10.1186/s40697-016-0105-x

Reading: **MT**, 2026-11-15. Main-text reading; figures as images and supplements not inspected; tables not comprehensively checked unless stated.

- **Role / population / unit:** matched home-daily-HD versus PD initiators, 2004–2011; person and matched pair. [reported Methods]
- **Sources:** provider treatment prescriptions linked to USRDS; other baseline/outcome variables obtained consistently from USRDS. Cause-of-death groups follow the 2012 Guide. [reported Methods]
- **Data needs:** index at home modality initiation, with matching on ESRD duration, age and era as well as propensity. Primary follow-up retains later switches; as-treated sensitivity attributes deaths for 90 days after switching. [reported Methods]
- **Pitfalls:** prevalent survivors dominate; CMS-2728 baseline may precede home initiation substantially. The statement that home-HD information is unavailable reflects this study's identification needs and vintage, not a universal current file rule. Methods caps follow-up at five years while Results reports a 7.2-year maximum; modality denominators also differ from the stated matched sample. [reported Methods; Results; inference]
- **Locators:** full prose main text; tables/supplements unread. Preserve modality history and specify switch grace periods rather than silently overwriting baseline exposure.
