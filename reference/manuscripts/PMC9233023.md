Reading: **MT**, 2026-11-15. Main-text reading; figures as images and supplements not inspected; tables not comprehensively checked unless stated.

- **Role / population / unit:** Medicare-primary/Part-D dialysis cohort with initiations 2004–2016; patient-year treatment rates during 2006–2016. [reported Methods]
- **Sources:** procedure CPT/ICD-9/ICD-10 codes and Part D SHPT medicines; ACS 2014 geography. [reported Methods]
- **Data needs:** distinct risk sets: surgery removes later surgical person-time but not medication follow-up. Censor death, transplant, coverage end or study end; preserve calendar-era comparisons. [reported Methods]
- **Pitfalls:** stated two-year dialysis lead-in is unclear for entrants after 2004. Annual “ever receiving” medication rates need exact numerator/risk-set clarification. Part D misses IV/bundled/OTC therapy, so no observed prescription is not no treatment. Later cinacalcet does not prove inadequate surgery. County versus ZIP/ZCTA poverty language needs reconciliation. [reported Methods; Limitations; inference]
- **Locators:** complete main text; procedure mapping and tables/supplements unread. PTH severity was unavailable; time to treatment is a proxy potentially influenced by access and practice. Do not import it as a biological severity measure.
