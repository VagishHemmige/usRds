Reading: **MT**, 2026-11-15. Main-text reading; figures as images and supplements not inspected; tables not comprehensively checked unless stated.

- **Role / population / unit:** USRDS failed-allograft cohort returning to dialysis, 1995–2015; person, matched pair and time-varying nephrectomy exposure. [reported Methods]
- **Files and fields:** patient profile, medical evidence, treatment history, transplant and hospitalization tables; CLM_FROM and CLM_THRU explicitly named. [reported Methods]
- **Data needs:** Medicare fee-for-service within 90 days after return; exclude graft survival <90 days, death within 30 days of failure, and retransplant within 30 days after nephrectomy. Baseline information spans up to three years; death competes with retransplant, while mortality follow-up continues after retransplant. [reported Methods; Discussion]
- **Pitfalls:** assigning every diagnosis/procedure to CLM_FROM is a convention, not proof of the actual event date. Eligibility defined by future survival/events and ever-exposed matching require careful temporal reconstruction despite time-varying exposure models. Indications for nephrectomy and uniform antibody data are unavailable. [reported Methods; Limitations; inference]
