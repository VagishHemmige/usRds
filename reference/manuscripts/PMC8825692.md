### PMC8825692

[Early steps to kidney transplantation among persons with HIV and end-stage renal disease in ESRD network 6.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8825692/) · 2022 · doi:10.1111/tid.13767

Reading: **MT**, 2026-11-15. Main-text reading; figures as images and supplements not inspected; tables not comprehensively checked unless stated.

- **Role / population / unit:** USRDS linkage to Southeastern Early Transplant Access Registry; incident dialysis 2012–2016, ages 18–70 in GA/NC/SC, followed through 2017; person/first transplant-process step. [reported Methods]
- **Sources:** CMS-2728, Part D, external referral/evaluation dates, waitlist, facility reports and ACS 2010–2014. HIV defined by antiretroviral prescription during study or attributed HIV nephropathy. [reported Data Sources]
- **Data needs:** require Part D and exclude preemptive referral/evaluation/listing; first dialysis facility assigned. Separate dialysis-to-referral, referral-to-evaluation and evaluation-to-listing time origins. Missing referral dates for downstream events are imputed by the paper. [reported Methods]
- **Pitfalls:** medication-based HIV classification can confuse hepatitis B treatment/PrEP and miss unobserved prescriptions. Imputed dates are not measured referrals; stage-specific denominators must remain distinct. Cause-specific versus subdistribution terminology is mixed and needs implementation verification; national referral capture cannot be assumed.
